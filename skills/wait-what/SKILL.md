description: "Stop. Dor is lost, in any context: find what on, check it, and explain it simply. Use when Dor says \"wait what\" or \"what's going on\", or a message did not land."
---

Wait, I don't understand. Say what actually happened and nothing on top of it.

1. **Find what Dor is lost on.** Dor's last messages point at it, in whatever context: your last message, a term, a result, an error, a decision, the work itself. When Dor's words name something, that is the whole scope. With nothing named, it is the **thread**: the work this conversation is on. With no work yet, ask Dor what Dor means.
2. **Check its real state.** Check only what it touches: files, git, live systems. A fact you cannot check is unknown. Change nothing.
3. **Re-pitch it.** Open with the point: where this leaves Dor and what it means. Each finding carries its consequence: what changes for Dor because of it. Cut whatever changes neither. Use plain words and the project's own names for things, as your own telling of it: what it is, what actually went on, and what it means for what Dor is doing, with the real results, numbers and names. When Dor is lost on the work itself, present it the way the `brief` skill does.
